02 · The checks

Six gates, run on every coin.

You never run these checks. The bot runs the same ones on every launch, every 15 seconds, with no dashboard and no command line on your side. The filters are tuned to reject: when data for a check is missing or unclear, the coin is skipped rather than guessed at.

  • 01

    The contract

    Mint and freeze authority must be renounced. Honeypot patterns and known risk code are rejected on sight.

    reject · mint authority live
  • 02

    The holders

    Top-10 concentration, insider and dev holdings, bundled buys, snipers, and fresh-wallet ratios are measured.

    reject · top-10 61%
  • 03

    The deployer

    The dev's launch history and rug ratio. Serial launchers and wallets with a record of abandoned tokens don't get through.

    reject · 4 of 7 rugs
  • 04

    The market

    Minimum market cap, liquidity, holders, and token age, plus a check for tokens still on the bonding curve.

    reject · still on the curve
  • 05

    The data

    If contract or market data is missing or unclear, the coin is skipped. Fail-closed by design.

    skip · data missing
  • 06

    The score

    Survivors are scored 0 to 100. Coins at or above the pass mark are posted once, then never re-alerted.

    pass · 87/100 → telegram
A hundred coins hit the gate. One reaches your phone.

New-coin feeds are mostly rugs, bundles, and wash volume. Chainseer runs the first pass for you and throws almost all of it away before it reaches your phone, wherever the day takes you.

Missing a good coin costs you one alert. Passing a bad one costs a portfolio. The thresholds are set accordingly, and the filter records how it did so the thresholds can be tightened where needed.

05 · Questions

Before you join.

Do I need to set anything up?

No. Joining the Telegram group is the entire setup. The bot already runs the screens and checks on its own schedule and posts the survivors as alerts; you decide what, if anything, to do with them.

Does Chainseer trade for me?

No. The bot only reads public market and on-chain data and never connects to a wallet, holds funds, signs transactions, or places orders. Every decision after an alert is yours.

Is this financial advice?

No. Chainseer publishes informational signals about on-chain activity. It does not know your circumstances, it cannot predict price, and a high score is not a recommendation to buy.

Which chain and data sources?

Solana only. Chainseer reads public token, holder, and security data from third-party market data providers and on-chain sources. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with Telegram or Solana.

How fast is an alert?

New coins are scanned every 15 seconds. Delivery depends on data-provider response time, rate limits, and Telegram, so treat timing as best-effort, not guaranteed.

Why do some tokens alert only once?

Alert-once is enabled by default and persisted in the database. It prevents repeat calls on the same token, even across bot restarts. Operators can change the cooldown behaviour through configuration.

Does a passing coin mean it's safe?

No. Chainseer filters out almost all rugs, but no filter catches everything. A pass means a coin cleared the checks, not that it is safe or will go up. Treat each alert as a shortlist and do your own research.

What does the score mean?

It is a 0 to 100 ranking of how cleanly a coin cleared the contract, holder, market, and deployer checks. It is a filter, not a probability of profit.

Do I need a wallet or an API key?

No. Followers only need Telegram. Any data-provider API keys are configured by the operator running the bot.
